Fetching & scope

i"Use site default" follows whatever the Settings page has set. Rendering a page in a real browser is much slower than a plain fetch and downloads that browser engine the first time it's ever used on this server.
iThis crawl ignores robots.txt unless checked.
i"Use site default" follows whatever the Settings page has set. "Seed's domain, including subdomains" follows e.g. blog.example.com and example.com from a seed of www.example.com, but not example.org. "Same domain, any subdomain, any top-level domain" is broader still -- from a seed of example.com it also follows example.org and www.example.de (the same domain name, any subdomain, under any TLD), but not other.com.
iOne domain per line. Always followed, even where "How far to follow links" above would otherwise reject them -- widens link scope, on top of it, rather than replacing it.
iOne domain per line. Never followed, even where link scope or allowed domains above would otherwise allow them -- blocked domains always win.
iWhen checked, a discovered link is also followed if its domain already has at least one indexed page, even outside link scope. Blocked domains above still override this.

Discovery

iWhen checked, /sitemap.xml is fetched from the seed's domain and every URL it lists is queued too.
iWhen checked, newly discovered pages are fetched before re-fetching ones already in the index, so a limited page budget goes toward new content first on a mostly-indexed site. Already-indexed pages are still crawled once every new one has been attempted.
